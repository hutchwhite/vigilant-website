## Top story

The Cyber AB used its July town hall to draw a line between the program and the pause. CMMC.com's recap, published July 29, reports that "the CMMC program itself has not been paused. What's on hold are the Phase 2 contractual requirements." The same recap says recommendations from the Reform Task Force are expected around early October 2026. C3PAOs can still issue Level 2 certifications, and contracts can still require Level 1 (Self) or Level 2 (Self).

## Rules and policy

- **Law firms read the memo the same way.** Latham & Watkins (July 30) notes that contracting officers must "amend active solicitations to explicitly remove those requirements and to modify existing contracts" that contain Level 2 (C3PAO) or Level 3 (DIBCAC) requirements. Holland & Knight's July 31 alert describes two memos: one from the CIO and one from the acquisition and sustainment office implementing it. *Why it matters:* expect contract modifications, not just a change in future solicitations.

## Industry and enforcement

- **Practitioner programs are being rebuilt.** At a July 30 forum for Registered Practitioner Organizations, the Cyber Engagement Forum (Cyber EF), "a wholly owned nonprofit subsidiary of The Cyber AB," laid out "a quarter-by-quarter plan to rebuild the CMMC practitioner program between now and mid-2027." It said "Training is being built to NIST SP 800-171 Rev 3." It also listed "A validation system for RPO services and products" for Q1 2027. *Why it matters:* consultant credentials are going to change. Ask any RPO you hire how it's preparing.
- **SBA Advocacy held its RFI roundtable on July 30.** The session was off the record.

## Still on the radar

- The RFI closes at 12:00 p.m. ET on August 14.
- No DOJ cyber-fraud settlements were announced this week.

## Watch list

- The task force's review timeline: 60 days from July 13.
- Contract modifications removing Level 2 (C3PAO) language.

## What to do this week

- If you hold or are pursuing a Level 2 certification, keep going. The program continues, and a certification still counts with primes.
- Read the next modification to your contract before you sign it.
- If you're responding to the RFI, you have two weeks.
