## Top story

A third straight quiet week. We found no new DoW CIO memo, class deviation, Federal Register action, Cyber AB press release, or DOJ cyber-fraud settlement dated October 19–23. The CMMC Reform Task Force report is still not public. The 60-day review closed around September 11, and Cyber AB CEO Matt Travis's personal estimate at the September town hall was "the back half of October at the earliest." We are now in that window. Travis also noted that approved recommendations would go through General Counsel, the Small Business office, OMB and the White House before public release, so a further wait would not be surprising.

## Rules and policy

- **Revision 3 still governs contract actions.** DFARS Class Deviation `2026-O0025`, Revision 3, signed Sept. 3, directs contracting officers to "remove or revise the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) requirements in new and existing solicitations and contracts" per the DoW CIO's July 13 memo. That memo permits Level 1 (Self) or Level 2 (Self) and requires baseline NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 compliance under DFARS `252.204-7012`. Where a contract still includes a CMMC level, `252.204-7021` still requires a current self-assessment posted in SPRS. We found no Revision 4. *Why it matters:* your implementation obligations did not pause, only third-party verification did.

## Cyber AB and C3PAOs

- **No October town hall posted yet.** The Cyber AB's town hall page lists sessions through September 2026. *Why it matters:* the next town hall is the likeliest place for an update on assessor renewals and how the ecosystem responds to the task force report.

## Industry and enforcement

- **Microsoft 365 G7 reaches GCC, not GCC High.** CMMC.com's October 22 explainer notes G7 (G5 plus Microsoft 365 Copilot, Entra Suite and Agent 365) became available in GCC on October 1, with GCC High and DoD not yet available. Directions on Microsoft (Oct. 14) expects GCC High and DoD availability closer to the end of 2026. The CMMC.com piece also cautions that "No license makes an organization CMMC compliant on its own." *Why it matters:* if your CUI, especially ITAR or EAR data, lives in GCC High, plan AI rollouts around that tier's timeline and configure the controls yourself.
- **No new DOJ cyber-fraud settlements announced Oct 19–23.** Honeywell Aerospace (Sept. 1, $2,042,518) remains the latest. DOJ notes "the claims resolved by the settlement are allegations only and there has been no determination of liability." *Why it matters:* your SPRS score is still a representation the government can test.

## Still on the radar

- **FY2027 NDAA.** Holland & Knight reported on October 9 that the House passed its version in July, while in the Senate "the Senate failed to invoke cloture on the motion to proceed to the bill." The Senate committee bill includes a CMMC grant program for Level 2 (C3PAO) assessment costs. Final action now looks like a lame-duck item.
- **CAICO renewal window opens November 1.** It closes December 31. Practitioners certified before January 1, 2026 "need at least 20 CPEs by December 31, 2026."

## Watch list

- Public release of the task force recommendations, and any Phase 2 restart date.
- Any Revision 4 to `2026-O0025` or proposed changes to 32 CFR Part 170.
- An October or November Cyber AB town hall.

## What to do this week

- Pull your SPRS entry and confirm the score and assessment date still match your current System Security Plan.
- CCPs, CCAs and LCCAs: tally your CPEs now so renewal is ready when the window opens Sunday.
- Before enabling Copilot or agent features in a CUI tenant, document how they fit your access control, audit and media protection practices.
