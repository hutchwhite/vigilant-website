## Top story

Another quiet week. We found no new DoW CIO memo, class deviation, Federal Register action, Cyber AB press release, or DOJ cyber-fraud settlement dated October 12–16. The CMMC Reform Task Force report is still not public, now more than a month after the 60-day review closed on September 11. The latest public estimate remains Cyber AB CEO Matt Travis's personal guess at the September town hall: "the back half of October at the earliest." Until something lands, the Phase 2 pause and DFARS Class Deviation `2026-O0025`, Revision 3 define the rules.

## Rules and policy

- **Revision 3 still governs contract actions.** The deviation, signed Sept. 3, directs contracting officers to "remove or revise the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) requirements in new and existing solicitations and contracts" per the DoW CIO's July 13 memo. That memo permits Level 1 (Self) or Level 2 (Self) and requires baseline NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 compliance under DFARS `252.204-7012`. Where a contract still includes a CMMC level, `252.204-7021` still requires a current self-assessment posted in SPRS. *Why it matters:* the pause changed who verifies you, not what you must implement.

## Industry and enforcement

- **Redspin survey coverage widened.** Corporate Compliance Insights' October 13 roundup reported the Redspin findings we covered last week, adding that almost 22% of respondents are slowing or halting CMMC efforts and about 20% have paused certification spending. *Why it matters:* most of your peers are still investing, so a long pause on your side widens the gap.
- **No new DOJ cyber-fraud settlements announced Oct 12–16.** The 2026 cases remain LOGZONE (June 18, $507,144) and Honeywell Aerospace (Sept. 1, $2,042,518). In LOGZONE, DOJ said a Defense Contract Management Agency assessment of its NIST SP 800-171 implementation "resulted in LOGZONE receiving a score of -170, at the low end of the possible score range of -203 to 110." Both releases note the claims are allegations only. *Why it matters:* the score you post in SPRS is a representation, and a government assessment can test it.

## Still on the radar

- **FY2027 NDAA.** The House passed H.R. 8800 on July 22. As of CRS's September 1 update, the Senate had not passed S. 4784. The Senate committee bill would create a CMMC grant program, capped at $50 million and up to $100,000 per recipient, for direct costs of a Level 2 (C3PAO) assessment.
- **CAICO renewal window opens November 1.** It closes December 31, per the Cyber AB's September town hall. Practitioners certified before January 1, 2026 need at least 20 CPEs by December 31.

## Watch list

- Public release of the task force recommendations, and whether the DoW CIO publishes them in full.
- Any Phase 2 restart date, or proposed changes to 32 CFR Part 170 or DFARS `252.204-7021`.
- The next Cyber AB town hall.

## What to do this week

- Re-score your NIST SP 800-171 self-assessment against your current evidence, not last year's. Update SPRS if the number changed.
- If you hold CCP, CCA or LCCA credentials, confirm your CPE count before the November 1 renewal window opens.
- Keep your POA&M moving. Whatever replaces Phase 2 will start from `252.204-7012`.
