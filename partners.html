What the mock assessment covers

Scope

All 110 Level 2 requirements

Each NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 requirement is evaluated against its assessment objectives in NIST SP 800-171A, following the phases of the CMMC Assessment Process: scoping review, document review, interviews, and examination of evidence.

Deliverable

Findings in assessment language

A written report rating every requirement MET or NOT MET, with the evidence gaps, SSP and scoping observations, and a plain readiness summary for the client's leadership.

Assessor

Led by an LCCA

Hutch White, Lead CMMC Certified Assessor and CISSP, runs every mock assessment personally. Remote by default, on-site available across Alaska.

Level 1

Level 1 checks too

For FCI-only clients, a shorter review against the 15 basic safeguarding requirements in FAR 52.240-93 before they sign their annual affirmation.

Independence

What a mock assessment is not

  • It is not a CMMC certification assessment. No results are entered in eMASS or SPRS, and it does not produce a CMMC status.
  • It does not guarantee the outcome of a C3PAO assessment.
  • It is advisory work. Under CMMC's conflict-of-interest rules, Hutch will not serve on the certification assessment of any organization he has mock-assessed.

How it works with partners

  1. Readiness is done

    Your team completes implementation and documentation with the client: SSP, policies, POA&M, asset inventory and network diagrams.

  2. Scoping call

    A short call with you and the client confirms the assessment scope, the CUI boundary, and the evidence to prepare.

  3. Document review

    Hutch reviews the SSP and supporting documents remotely against the assessment objectives.

  4. Interviews and evidence

    Interviews with the people who run the controls, and examination of configurations and records. Remote or on-site in Alaska.

  5. Findings debrief

    A walkthrough of results with you and the client, followed by the written report.

  6. Remediation and re-check

    Your team closes the gaps. An optional targeted re-check covers only the requirements that were NOT MET.

Why partners use it

  • Independent eyes before the real onesFind the gaps a C3PAO would find while there is still time to fix them.
  • Protects your reputationA failed certification reflects on the team that did the readiness work. A mock assessment lowers that risk.
  • The client stays yoursVigilant reports findings to you and the client together, and you lead the remediation.
  • Local and practitioner-ledAn Anchorage-based LCCA who can be on-site, not a national firm fitting you into a queue.
