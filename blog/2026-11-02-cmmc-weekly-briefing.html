CMMC Weekly Briefing · Oct 26–30, 2026

CMMC task force summary, obtained by FNN, floats a narrower Phase 2 restart

Published November 2, 2026 · Compiled by Vigilant Cybersecurity

Top story

The week's only real news came from reporting, not from DoD. In a two-part investigation (Oct. 26 and 27), Federal News Network's Jason Miller reported on the CMMC Reform Task Force's RFI results and published options from a task force summary FNN obtained. DoD declined to comment on 15 specific questions, and no memo, rule or deviation followed. Treat everything below as options under discussion, not decisions.

  • The RFI numbers. DoD reviewed 997 responses, 509 of them from the defense industrial base. FNN reports DoD cited an "average assessment cost ($47,000)," compared with SBA Administrator Kelly Loeffler's July statement that compliance "could cost" as much as $600,000. Why it mattersThe cost case for the pause looks weaker than it did in July.
  • Assessor capacity. As of September, 2,433 companies held Level 2 (C3PAO) certifications. An unnamed industry source told FNN that "There has never once during all of Phase 1 where ecosystem utilized more than half of the available assessment capacity." Why it mattersThis cuts against the July memo's claim of "severe shortages in third-party assessment capacity."
  • The 75-day review is past 100 days. DoD allotted 60 days for feedback and 15 for a report. No report has been released. Why it mattersContractors still have no restart date to plan around.

Rules and policy

  • Short-term options in the task force summary. Per FNN, the roughly 90-day options are: restart Phase 2 "with a narrower population" while longer-term changes go through rulemaking; work with the Cyber AB and CAICO on assessor and consultant guidance; publish assessor availability statistics; and expand POA&M eligibility to 270 days, which FNN says requires rulemaking. Longer-term ideas include ISO 27001 crediting, reducing the CUI flowed to primes and subs, and OSCAL-based evidence criteria. Why it mattersIf Phase 2 restarts, it may not apply to everyone at once.
  • NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 3. FNN reports the task force recommends finalizing the move to Rev. 3, a transition FNN says has been on hold since Kirsten Davies became CIO. An industry source said a Rev. 3 move is unlikely before the FAR CUI rule takes effect. Why it mattersRev. 2 remains your baseline for now.
  • CUI marking. The summary says respondents flagged inconsistent CUI marking, and DoD says a planned FAR rule could add a standard form defining the CUI shared under a contract. Why it mattersBetter marking could shrink the set of contracts and systems in scope.
  • Revision 3 still governs contracts. DFARS Class Deviation 2026-O0025, Revision 3 (Sept. 3), directs contracting officers to "remove or revise the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) requirements in new and existing solicitations and contracts." We found no Revision 4 and no new Federal Register CMMC action. Why it mattersDFARS 252.204-7012 and NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 obligations still apply. Where a contract includes a CMMC level, 252.204-7021 still requires a current SPRS self-assessment.

Cyber AB and C3PAOs

  • Assessment firms under strain. Hunton partner Eric Crusius told FNN many C3PAOs are "suffering, laying people off and even potentially going out of business." The Cyber AB declined to comment. Why it mattersIf you plan a Level 2 (C3PAO) assessment, confirm your assessor's capacity and staffing before you sign.

Industry and enforcement

  • No new DOJ cyber-fraud settlements announced Oct 26–30. Honeywell Aerospace (Sept. 1, $2,042,518) remains the latest. Why it mattersThe pause did not change your exposure on what you report in SPRS.

Still on the radar

  • FY2027 NDAA. As of Holland & Knight's Oct. 9 update, the Senate had not advanced its bill, which includes a CMMC grant program for Level 2 (C3PAO) assessment costs.
  • FAR CUI rule. Both the Rev. 3 timing and the CUI marking fix point to it.

Watch list

  • Public release of the task force report, or a Phase 2 restart announcement.
  • Any Revision 4 to 2026-O0025, or rulemaking for 270-day POA&Ms.
  • An October or November Cyber AB town hall with official figures.

What to do this week

  • Keep implementing NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2. A narrower restart could still include you.
  • Ask your primes and contracting officers which CUI they actually send you, and document it. That shapes your scope today and under any FAR CUI rule.
  • If you plan a C3PAO assessment, confirm the firm's schedule and stability in writing.
