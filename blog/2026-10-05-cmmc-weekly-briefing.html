CMMC Weekly Briefing · Sep 28–Oct 2, 2026

Task force report now "back half of October at the earliest," Cyber AB says

Published October 5, 2026 · Compiled by Vigilant Cybersecurity

Top story

The CMMC Reform Task Force report is still not public, and the Cyber AB now expects it later than many had hoped. In the Cyber AB's September town hall, recapped September 30, CEO Matt Travis said the task force's 60-day review has ended and it is "in the roughly 15-day window for drafting recommendations." He estimated public release in "the back half of October at the earliest," after reviews by the General Counsel's office, the Small Business office, OMB and the White House, and stressed he had no confirmed timeline. We found no new DoW CIO memo, class deviation, or Federal Register action on CMMC during the week.

Rules and policy

  • No new CMMC rule actions this week. DFARS Class Deviation 2026-O0025, Revision 3 (signed Sept. 3) remains the operative instruction. It directs contracting officers to "remove or revise the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) requirements in new and existing solicitations and contracts" per the DoW CIO's July 13 memo, which permits Level 1 (Self) or Level 2 (Self) and requires baseline NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 compliance under DFARS 252.204-7012. Why it mattersUntil the task force report lands, Revision 3 is what shapes your next contract modification.
  • "DFARS 7012 requirements remain in force." That line came from Travis's opening remarks at the town hall. Why it mattersThe Phase 2 pause did not pause safeguarding obligations, incident reporting, or SPRS accuracy.

Cyber AB and C3PAOs

  • Certifications keep climbing during the pause. Per the town hall recap, final Level 2 (C3PAO) certificates reached 2,362, up 12% from August. Conditional certificates rose 7% to 71, and Level 2 assessments in progress dipped 5% to 151. Authorized or accredited C3PAOs rose to 117, four of them now fully accredited to ISO/IEC 17020. Why it mattersPrimes and some contractors are still choosing third-party certification even where it isn't contractually required.
  • CAICO renewal window opens November 1. The window closes December 31, renewals process January 1, and a 30-day grace period runs through January 30. Practitioners certified before January 1, 2026 need "at least 20 CPEs by December 31, 2026," and 90 of the 120 cycle CPEs must be domain-specific. Why it mattersCCAs and LCCAs supporting your assessment need current credentials.
  • Survey shows support for the program, split on the pause. Travis presented ISC2 survey results: "76% of respondents agreed that a CMMC program is necessary," while "42% disagreed with pausing Phase 2, while 38% supported it." Why it mattersIndustry sentiment favors fixing CMMC, not ending it.
  • Reform talk was labeled speculation. Speakers said they had "no inside information on the task force's work." Travis emphasized preserving "the value of existing Level 2 certificates," possibly through solicitation credits. Why it mattersDon't make spending decisions on town hall speculation.

Industry and enforcement

  • No new DOJ cyber-fraud settlements announced Sep 28–Oct 2. Why it mattersQuiet weeks don't change the risk. See the Honeywell matter below.

Still on the radar

  • Honeywell Aerospace settlement (Sept. 1, 2026). Honeywell Aerospace Inc. agreed to pay $2,042,518 to resolve allegations it "failed to comply with cybersecurity requirements specified in" NIST SP 800-171 from April 2020 through December 2023. A former employee who filed the whistleblower suit received $375,823. Correction: last week's briefing called the June LOGZONE settlement the most recent we were tracking; Honeywell came later.

Watch list

  • Release of the task force recommendations, now expected late October at the earliest.
  • Any Phase 2 restart date or proposed changes to 32 CFR Part 170 or DFARS 252.204-7021.
  • Contract modifications under Revision 3 removing or revising CMMC requirements.

What to do this week

  • Confirm your SPRS score and annual affirmation are current and match your actual implementation.
  • If your team holds CCP, CCA or LCCA credentials, check CPE status before the November 1 renewal window.
  • Keep working NIST SP 800-171 gaps. The task force can change the assessment model, not 252.204-7012.
