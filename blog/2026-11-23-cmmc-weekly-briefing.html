CMMC Weekly Briefing · Nov 16–20, 2026

Johnson Controls settles cyber-fraud claims for $5M as Phase 2 stays paused

Published November 23, 2026 · Compiled by Vigilant Cybersecurity

Top story

  • Johnson Controls agrees to pay $5 million. DOJ announced that HVAC supplier Johnson Controls Inc. and two subsidiaries agreed to pay $5 million "to Settle Cybersecurity Fraud Allegations." Coverage describes the claims as "alleged security plan gaps in federal contracts." The announcement came in mid-November, just before last week, and we had not covered it. These are allegations, resolved by settlement. Why it mattersIt is the second cyber False Claims Act resolution this month, and both turn on paperwork any small contractor can check: the system security plan and what you report to SPRS.

Rules and policy

  • Nothing new from DoW last week. We found no new DoW CIO memo, no Federal Register action and no revision to DFARS Class Deviation 2026-O0025. Revision 3 (Sept. 3) still directs contracting officers to "remove or revise the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) requirements in new and existing solicitations and contracts." Why it mattersDFARS 252.204-7012 and NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 still apply, and where a contract still includes a CMMC level, 252.204-7021 still requires a current SPRS self-assessment.
  • No restart date. Phase 2 has been suspended since July 13, and the CMMC Reform Task Force report has not been released. Why it mattersA restart could come with short notice, so keep your Level 2 preparation moving.

Cyber AB and C3PAOs

  • Voluntary certifications keep coming. Horizon3.ai announced CMMC Level 2 certification on Nov. 19, and Interloc Solutions announced "Final CMMC Level 2 Certification." Why it mattersVendors that sell into the defense industrial base still treat Level 2 (C3PAO) certification as a selling point even though solicitations no longer require it.

Industry and enforcement

  • Same theory, two cases. The Johnson Controls settlement follows ILC Dover and ILC Astrospace's $13 million settlement earlier this month over "alleged cyber compliance failures in Army, MDA, NASA contracts." Why it mattersDOJ is pursuing NIST SP 800-171 attestations whether or not CMMC certification is in force.

Still on the radar

  • CCP and CCA renewals. Per the Cyber AB's September town hall, the renewal window that opened Nov. 1 closes Dec. 31, with a grace period through Jan. 30.
  • FY2027 NDAA. We found no new Senate action, so the proposed small-business CMMC grant provision remains pending.

Watch list

  • Release of the task force report or a Phase 2 restart announcement.
  • Any Revision 4 to 2026-O0025.
  • The Dec. 31 CCP and CCA renewal deadline.

What to do this week

  • Pull out your system security plan and confirm it describes the system you actually run today.
  • Check that your SPRS score is current and matches what you can document. If you have open POA&M items, make sure each has an owner and a date.
  • If a cloud service holds your CUI, file its FedRAMP Moderate authorization or equivalency evidence with your SSP.
