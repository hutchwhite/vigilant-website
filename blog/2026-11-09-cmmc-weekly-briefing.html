CMMC Weekly Briefing · Nov 2–6, 2026

Quiet week for CMMC: task force report still out, Phase 2 still paused

Published November 9, 2026 · Compiled by Vigilant Cybersecurity

Top story

Nothing official moved on CMMC between Nov. 2 and Nov. 6. We found no new DoW CIO memo, no revision to the class deviation, no Federal Register action, no Cyber AB press release and no new DOJ cyber-fraud settlement.

  • The task force report is still unpublished. The CMMC Reform Task Force's options are known only through Federal News Network's Oct. 27 reporting on a summary FNN obtained, which was dated early September. DoD has not released the report or adopted any option. Why it mattersNearly four months after the July 13 suspension, contractors still have no restart date to plan around.

Rules and policy

  • Revision 3 still governs contracts. DFARS Class Deviation 2026-O0025, Revision 3 (Sept. 3), directs contracting officers to "remove or revise the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) requirements in new and existing solicitations and contracts." Inside Government Contracts notes the suspension "does not affect the underlying requirements to comply with NIST SP 800-171 Revision 2." Why it mattersDFARS 252.204-7012 obligations are unchanged, and where a contract still includes a CMMC level, 252.204-7021 still requires a current SPRS self-assessment.
  • Options on the table, not decisions. Per FNN, the summary says that "Over a 90-day period," DoD "could restart Phase 2 of CMMC with a narrower population," and that the CIO "could expand POA&M eligibility to 270 days," which would require rulemaking. It also recommends "finalizing the move to NIST SP 800-171 Revision 3." Why it mattersA restart may phase in by population, so you cannot assume you are out of scope.

Cyber AB and C3PAOs

  • CCP and CCA renewal window is open. Per the Cyber AB's September town hall (recap published Sept. 30), certifications are moving to a Jan. 1 calendar-year cycle: "The renewal window opens November 1 and closes December 31," with a 30-day grace period through Jan. 30. CCPs and CCAs certified before Jan. 1, 2026 "need at least 20 CPEs by December 31, 2026," and "CCAs must also maintain an underlying DoD 8140 certification at the intermediate or advanced level." Why it mattersIf you rely on an in-house CCP or an outside CCA, confirm they renew on time.
  • The program itself continues. Cyber AB CEO Matthew Travis said at that town hall that "the CMMC program itself is not paused"; only Phase 2 contract requirements were. The recap reported 2,362 final Level 2 certificates and 117 authorized or accredited C3PAOs. Why it mattersVoluntary Level 2 (C3PAO) assessments are still available if a prime asks for one.

Industry and enforcement

  • No new DOJ cyber-fraud settlements announced Nov. 2–6. Honeywell Aerospace (Sept. 1, $2,042,518) remains the latest. DOJ alleged a business unit failed "to comply with cybersecurity requirements specified in National Institute of Standards and Technology" standards; the release notes these are allegations only. Why it mattersThe pause did not change your exposure on what you attest to in SPRS.

Still on the radar

  • FY2027 NDAA. The House passed H.R. 8800 on July 22. In the Senate, cloture on the motion to proceed to S. 4784 failed July 14, and CRS's Sept. 1 update reported no further steps. We found no newer Senate action, so the proposed small-business CMMC grant provision remains pending.
  • FAR CUI rule. FNN reports a planned FAR rule would create a standard form defining the CUI shared under a contract. It is not yet published.

Watch list

  • Public release of the task force report or a Phase 2 restart announcement.
  • Any Revision 4 to 2026-O0025, or rulemaking for 270-day POA&Ms.

What to do this week

  • Re-run your NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 self-assessment if it is more than a few months old, and make sure the SPRS score matches what you can document.
  • If you employ a CCP or CCA, check their CPE count now; the renewal window closes Dec. 31.
  • Ask your primes which CUI they actually send you, and write it down.
